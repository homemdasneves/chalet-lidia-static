House rules, wifi access, and check-in/check-out times for a smooth stay.

House Rules
  • your host lives on the same location of your rental
  • outside areas (garden and swimming pool) are shared with other guests and hosts;
  • the indoor space is private for you only
  • respect night time
  •  no parties or extra guests are allowed
  • swimming pool is not fenced: children must be supervised
  • there are 3 security cameras outside in common passage areas 

We’re sorry if you are uncomfortable with any of the above. In that case, please cancel your reservation.

Wifi

The network name and password are displayed near the TV in the living room. If you can't find them, just message your host and we'll send them right over.

Check-in / Check-out

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is until 11:00 AM. Contact your host if you need a different time — we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Trash

There's a regular trash container and recycling container right accross the street on the sidewalk on the train station side.
When you leave the property just cross to the train station side and go left.
