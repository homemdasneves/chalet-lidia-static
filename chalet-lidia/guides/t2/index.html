Find your Lodge
  • after the main gate turn left and then go downstairs.
  • when you are downstairs you will see 2 glass doors, yours is on the right.
  • to access your lodge insert the same code from the main gate (again press # in the end)
  • when you press each number a small green light will light up in the top right corner
  • if the code is correct the small green light blinks and you'll hear the lock unlocking (please wait until it finishes)
  • when it's unlocked please turn the handle counter-clockwise until it stays in vertical position, then push the door inside and slide it to the right.

Lock from outside
  • close the door by sliding it to the right and pull the handle towards you
  • then rotate the handle counter-clockwise until its pointing down
  • press the # button (bottom right) for 3 to 4 seconds until you hear the noise of the locker

 

Lock from inside
  • close the door by sliding it to the right and push it away from you
  • then rotate the handle counter-clockwise until its pointing to the right
  • finally press the button on the face of the smart lock - it will spin automatically and lock the handle

 

Unlock from outside
  • to access your lodge insert your access code and finish with the # button (bottom right)
  • when you press each number a small green light will light up in the top right corner
  • if the code is correct the small green light blinks and you'll hear the lock unlocking
  • when it's unlocked please turn the handle clockwise until it's pointing up, then push the door inside and slide it to the right.

 

Unlock from inside
  • push the button on the face of the smart lock
  • it will spin automatically and unlock the handle 
  • rotate the handle clockwise, until its pointing to the left
  • pull the door towards you and then slide it to the left to open
