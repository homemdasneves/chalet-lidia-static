Find your Lodge
  • after the main gate turn left and then go downstairs.
  • when you are downstairs you will see 2 glass doors, the suite lodge is on the left.
  • to access your lodge insert the same code from the main gate (again press # in the end)
  • when you press each number a small green light will light up in the top right corner
  • if the code is correct the small green light blinks and you'll hear the lock unlocking (please wait until it finishes)
  • when it's unlocked please turn the handle to the left and up so the handle stays in vertical position, then push the door inside and slide to the left.

Unlock from outside
  • insert your acess code and finish with the # button

Close Blackout Shutters
  • there's a remote hanging on the wall between the rooms
  • press down to close and up to open
  • you can choose to control both shutters or each one at a time, by clicking on the button at the bottom of the remote (with a dash "—")
